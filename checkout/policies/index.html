Last updated September 19, 2026. These policies describe the website edition and the planned Mac App Store edition. Checkout remains unavailable until the signed private release is complete.

Offer and payment

The website edition is sold as a one-time purchase at the price shown by Stripe, plus applicable tax. There is no recurring Screencast.to subscription. Stripe processes payment information; Screencast.to does not receive or store full card details.

Your purchase permits continued use of the downloaded version. It does not promise every future product, paid service, or major feature. Prices may change for future purchasers.

When checkout is marked Test mode, no real payment is accepted and no commercial purchase is created.

Private download

After Stripe confirms payment, the server places the checkout reference in a necessary 30-day, host-only, secure, HttpOnly purchase cookie and redirects to a clean confirmation address. The confirmation page never exposes the checkout reference to page scripts.

Each download request rechecks the payment, product, quantity, and allowed price with Stripe. Approved requests stream the current installer from private Cloudflare R2 storage through Screencast.to. The installer is not placed in the public website, source repository, or a public GitHub Release.

The cookie allows this browser to return to the confirmation page for 30 days. Clearing cookies or changing browsers removes that convenience but does not limit the installed app. Contact support using the purchase email if access must be restored.

Website-edition updates

The website edition uses Sparkle 2 to check for signed updates. Its feed and update archive are served from private Cloudflare R2 storage through Screencast.to endpoints. Official builds carry an update-access token in addition to Sparkle’s EdDSA public key. That token keeps the updater path out of ordinary public browsing, but it is not represented as copy protection or an unextractable secret.

Sparkle verifies each update’s EdDSA signature, and releases are also signed with Apple Developer ID and notarized. Update requests include ordinary delivery information such as IP address and app version. Sparkle system-profile reporting is disabled.

Mac App Store and Productivity Bundle

The website edition and Mac App Store edition are separate purchases. Website purchases do not create Mac App Store ownership; App Store purchases do not create access to the website’s Stripe download.

Apple handles payment, download, refunds, and updates for the Mac App Store edition. That build contains no Sparkle framework or website update token. The planned Productivity Bundle is an Apple-only bundle of three independently useful apps, including Screencast.to and Yaprflow. Its final availability, member apps, and bundle price will be shown by Apple.

Mac app privacy

Screen capture, camera and microphone capture, system-audio capture, recording, playback, and local file management happen on the Mac. Recordings are not uploaded merely because they were created. The app has no advertising SDK and does not require a Screencast.to account.

macOS permission prompts control Screen Recording, camera, and microphone access. You can change them in System Settings. The core recorder remains useful without hosted sharing.

Website purchase data

Stripe retains the payment record and processes the information needed for checkout and fraud prevention. Cloudflare processes website requests and privately stores release files. Screencast.to uses the checkout session reference only to verify purchase status and deliver the installer. This site includes no advertising or behavioral analytics scripts.

Optional temporary sharing

Uploading is a separate, explicit action. The first-party App Store sharing service verifies an anonymous Apple-signed purchase proof, then issues a short-lived upload authorization. Shared links are unlisted but accessible to anyone who has the random URL. Uploaded files are normally deleted by storage lifecycle rules within 24–48 hours.

The website edition does not receive access to the App Store-funded sharing service by purchasing through Stripe. Public source builds keep hosted sharing disabled unless a developer configures infrastructure they control.

Refunds and support

For website purchase, refund, or download questions, email support@screencast.to from the address used at checkout. Describe the problem and do not send card details. A fixed direct-sale refund window has not yet been published and must be decided before live checkout is enabled.

For Mac App Store billing or refunds, use Apple’s purchase-support process because Apple is the seller of record for that edition.

Source license

Current project-owned source is available for noncommercial use under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. Historical grants remain in force for the snapshots they covered. The source license is separate from the website purchase and does not grant rights to Screencast.to branding beyond applicable law and the repository trademark policy.
